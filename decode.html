The Secret Code of Victorian Bouquets

In the Victorian era, expressing deep affection openly was considered scandalous. High-society courtiers developed a sophisticated secret code known as tussie-mussies or talking bouquets. A red rose pledged enduring passion, baby's breath promised celestial innocence, while a sprig of lavender whispered tranquility and peace.

"To hand someone a blossom was to hand them the unspoken thoughts of your heart."
